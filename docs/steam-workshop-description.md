[h1]Geographic Labels[/h1]

[b]Civilization VI–style place names painted across the Civilization VII map.[/b]

Writes the names of the land and the water back onto the map: continents, islands, deserts, mountain ranges, taiga, jungle and natural wonders, plus seas, bays, gulfs, fjords, reefs, lakes and the rivers themselves. Land and region names are [b]period-accurate and drawn per civilization[/b]: the nearest civ lends its own real, era-appropriate place names, so a range by Rome reads [i]Zagrus Mountains[/i] and an island off Greece [i]Isle of Creta[/i], shifting across the ages as new civs rise. Rivers, seas, continents and wonders use the game's own names. Don't like a name? [b]Rename it[/b], and it's saved with your game.

Off by default: tick [b]Geographic Names[/b] in the mini-map's Decorations list, next to [i]Yields[/i].

[h2]On the map[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Land:[/b] continents, islands, deserts, mountain ranges, taiga, jungle and natural wonders, as soft, translucent labels.
[*][b]Water and coast:[/b] seas, gulfs, bays, sounds, inlets, fjords, reefs, atolls, estuaries, archipelagos, keys and lakes, classified by each basin's size, shape and how enclosed it is by land.
[*][b]Rivers[/b], navigable and minor, named by the game and laid along their course.
[*][b]About 1,200 real place names[/b] across all 30 civilizations (Latin for Rome, Ancient Greek for Greece, Quechua for the Inca, and so on), plus a neutral pool for unclaimed land.
[*][b]A clear map hierarchy:[/b] continents and seas largest, rivers smallest.
[*]Labels show only where you have explored.
[/list]

[h2]Rename Places[/h2]
Click [b]Rename Places…[/b] under the Geographic Names checkbox. A window lists every place in your game with its type and an editable name.
[list]
[*][b]Search[/b] by name or by kind: type "reef" to list every reef.
[*][b]Sort[/b] by type or by name: click a column title, and click again to reverse it.
[*]Type a new name and press Enter, or just click away. Your names are marked with a ★ and win when labels overlap.
[*][b]Restore[/b] (or a blank name) brings back the generated name.
[*]Names are saved with the game and carry across ages.
[*]Works with a controller: the on-screen keyboard opens for editing.
[/list]

[h2]Options[/h2]
In [b]Options → Add-Ons → Geographic Labels[/b]:
[list]
[*]Show or hide each kind of label, grouped into four sections you can fold away: Land, Island, Sea and Coast, and River and Lake Labels. Changes show on the map right away, and Cancel Changes puts them back.
[*][b]Follow the terrain:[/b] lays names flat and turns them along ranges, coasts and rivers, the way Civilization VI did.
[/list]

[b]Compatibility:[/b] additive and observer-based, with no base UI files replaced, so it coexists with other mods. Works in new games and in games already in progress. Names are stored per save.

[b]Languages:[/b] English.
